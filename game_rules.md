### Game Rules

#### Deck
  - The deck contains 60 cards in total.
  - Each card has a suit: spades, hearts, diamonds, and clubs.
  - Each suit contains cards numbered from 1 to 15.

#### Game Setup
  - Determine the number of players: 2-5 persons.
  - Determine the number of rounds:  
    The total number of rounds is calculated as  
    **(Total number of cards - 1) / number of players**, rounded down.
  - One game consists of several rounds, and one round may have several tricks.

#### Each Round
  - Shuffle the deck.
  - Determine the starting player and playing order (randomly).
  - Deal cards to each player according to the determined playing order. In n-th round each player receives n cards.
  - Reveal the top card from the remaining deck and read its suit; The suit of this card becomes the **bonus suit** for the whole round.

#### Each Trick in round
  - **Playing cards:** the current starting player plays 1 card first.  
    The other players then play 1 card each according to the playing order.  
    Each player may play only 1 card per trick.
  - **Winner Determination:** the player who plays the highest number wins the trick.  
                          If multiple players play the same highest number, a card with the **bonus suit** takes priority.  
                          If none of the tied highest cards belongs to the bonus suit, the player who played that highest card first wins the trick.
  - **Scoring:**
      - Winning the trick gives **2 points**.
      - Playing a card of the **bonus suit** gives **1 point**.
      - If a player both wins the trick and plays a card of the bonus suit, they receive **3 points**.
  - **Next trick:** within each round, the starting player rotates according to the fixed playing order；  
                    In each new trick, the next player in that order starts.

#### End of a Round
  - A round ends when all players have played all of their cards.
  - Record the total score of each player for that round.

### Game Winning
  - When all rounds end, the player with the highest points win the game.



    所有round结束后，总分最高的玩家胜利。
